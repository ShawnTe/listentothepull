What happens inside each session? the setlist

Each week, we gather for a guided Review, Align & Plan Session designed to help you start the week with clarity and presence. You’ll be guided through a process to:

  1. 01

    Arrive and settle

    We begin by creating a little space between you and the noise of the week.

  2. 02

    Reconnect with yourself

    Through a simple guided visualization, you’ll connect with your best self — the part of you that knows what matters, what’s next, and how you want to move.

  3. 03

    Review what’s important

    We’ll orient around your current projects, priorities, commitments, dates, and open loops.

  4. 04

    Plan from alignment

    Rather than forcing yourself into a rigid productivity plan, you’ll choose what deserves your attention from a grounded, intentional place.

  5. 05

    Leave with peace of mind

    You’ll begin the week knowing you’ve looked at the important stuff — and you’re moving forward with yourself, not against yourself.

Questions? Good.

What if I miss a Monday?

Every session is recorded, so you can catch up whenever it works for you.

Will I have to talk or share in front of the group?

Nope. The session is mostly silent — I guide, and you do your own reviewing and planning in your own space. You can share if you want to, but you never have to.

Do I need to be on camera?

Camera on or off — whatever feels right that day.

What if I want help with something specific?

At the end of each session there’s optional spot coaching. Stay if you want support with something, or head off into your week.

Do I need a specific project or goal?

No. You don’t need to have a specific project or program you’re working through — just a desire to stop letting the weeks blur by.
